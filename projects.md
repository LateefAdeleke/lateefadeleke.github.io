<div class="card resource-card"><div><h3>Uneme Documentation Project</h3><p><strong>ELDP-supported · Principal Investigator</strong></p><p>A multi-year documentation project creating naturalistic audiovisual recordings, targeted elicitation, annotations, grammatical analyses, lexical resources, and archival deposits focused on Uneme language, cultural practices, oral history, and indigenous knowledge.</p><div class="tag-row"><span class="tag">Language documentation</span><span class="tag">ELAR</span><span class="tag">Audiovisual corpus</span><span class="tag">Fieldwork</span></div></div></div>

<div class="card resource-card"><div><h3>Uneme Speech Corpus & ASR</h3><p>A speech corpus derived from documentary fieldwork and designed for experiments on low-resource automatic speech recognition. The research examines naturalistic versus constrained speech, training composition, cross-lingual transfer, tonal and segmental errors, and data-efficient adaptation.</p><div class="tag-row"><span class="tag">Whisper</span><span class="tag">XLS-R</span><span class="tag">ASR</span><span class="tag">Naturalistic speech</span></div></div></div>

<div class="card resource-card"><div><h3>Uneme–English Lexical Resources</h3><p>A multimedia lexical resource integrating field recordings, elicited lexical data, grammatical information, and corpus examples. The workflow uses FLEx alongside documentary annotations and is designed to support both community-oriented dictionary outputs and linguistic research.</p><div class="tag-row"><span class="tag">FLEx</span><span class="tag">Lexicography</span><span class="tag">Corpus examples</span><span class="tag">Multimedia</span></div></div></div>

<div class="card resource-card"><div><h3>Linguistically Informed ASR Evaluation</h3><p>Methods for analyzing speech-recognition behavior using language-specific phonological structure, tone, and contrastive features rather than relying only on aggregate word-level scores.</p><p><a href="https://aclanthology.org/2026.africanlp-main.14/">AfricaNLP 2026 paper →</a></p><div class="tag-row"><span class="tag">FER</span><span class="tag">Tone</span><span class="tag">Error analysis</span><span class="tag">African NLP</span></div></div></div>

<div class="card resource-card"><div><h3>Grammar & Variation in Edoid Languages</h3><p>Fieldwork- and corpus-based research on tense, aspect, negation, grammatical tone, focus, movement, and morphosyntactic variation in Uneme and related Edoid languages.</p><div class="tag-row"><span class="tag">TAM</span><span class="tag">Negation</span><span class="tag">Grammatical tone</span><span class="tag">Syntax</span></div></div></div>
