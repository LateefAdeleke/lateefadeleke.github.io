    <div class="section-head">
      <p class="kicker">Methodology</p>
      <h2>Documentation as research infrastructure</h2>
      <p class="lead">
        My fieldwork combines naturalistic documentation, targeted linguistic elicitation,
        lexical work, sociolinguistic interviews, procedural recordings, oral history,
        and cultural documentation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="grid-2">

      <article class="card">
        <h3>Primary Data Collection</h3>
        <p>
          I collect audiovisual data across a range of communicative settings,
          including spontaneous narratives, conversations, cultural activities,
          procedural demonstrations, oral histories, and targeted grammatical sessions.
        </p>

        <p>
          This allows the resulting corpus to represent both carefully controlled
          linguistic contrasts and the variability of naturally occurring speech.
        </p>

        <div class="tag-row">
          <span class="tag">Naturalistic speech</span>
          <span class="tag">Elicitation</span>
          <span class="tag">Oral history</span>
          <span class="tag">Procedural data</span>
          <span class="tag">Audiovisual recording</span>
        </div>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <h3>Annotation &amp; Resource Development</h3>
        <p>
          Field recordings are transformed into structured research resources through
          transcription, translation, annotation, lexical analysis, grammatical analysis,
          metadata management, and archival preparation.
        </p>

        <p>
          These materials support grammatical description, searchable corpora,
          lexical resources, community-facing outputs, and computational datasets.
        </p>

    <div class="section-head">
      <p class="kicker">Current field program</p>
      <h2>Uneme documentation</h2>
      <p class="lead">
        My current major field project focuses on Uneme, an Edoid language of Nigeria,
        with documentation spanning language use, grammatical variation, oral history,
        cultural practices, indigenous knowledge, and material culture.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="grid-3">

      <div class="card">
        <h3>Language</h3>
        <p>
          Naturalistic discourse, lexical data, grammatical elicitation,
          morphosyntax, tone, tense-aspect systems, negation, focus, and variation.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="card">
        <h3>Culture &amp; Knowledge</h3>
        <p>
          Oral history, migration narratives, festivals, traditional practices,
          material culture, blacksmithing, food production, and local knowledge systems.
        </p>
      </div>

    </div>
